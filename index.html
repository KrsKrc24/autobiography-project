A life span development reflection

About me

I was born in 2008 and have lived in Issaquah, Washington, my whole life. I grew up with my parents and my younger brother, who was born in 2012. My grandparents stayed with us for about six months each year. In my Indian family, visiting the temple and spending time with relatives were regular parts of life.

I have always liked asking questions and figuring things out. This project follows that curiosity through early childhood, middle childhood, and adolescence. Today, I lead International Children’s Wellness (ICW), a cancer prevention nonprofit I co-founded in 8th grade, and I do research in an immunology lab at Fred Hutch.

What I want to learn

I want to understand how my childhood connects to who I am today. Looking back can help me see how my family, culture, and surroundings shaped my interests. I also want to understand how my ways of learning have changed.

My theme

My theme is how my curiosity has grown. When I was little, I explored things by touching and looking at them. Later, I used books and science experiments to find answers. Now, I also learn by talking to people and thinking about my own behavior.

The course theme I am using is continuous versus discontinuous development. Continuous development means change that builds gradually over time. Discontinuous development describes change in distinct stages, as in Piaget’s theory. My story seems to show a gradual growth in how I learn, even though I have organized it into age groups. (Riser et al., 2024, Section 1.2)
